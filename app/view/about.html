About this project

A museum of Women's World Cup history, built as a web development project.

The idea

The Women's World Cup Database Museum is a database-driven website that collects the history of the Women's World Cup from the first edition in 1991 to today: every tournament, every national team and, in time, every player who has taken part.

The goal is to make the history of women's football easy to explore. Anyone can look up a team, a tournament or a player, and find out how the competition grew from 12 teams to 32.

What is in the museum now

  • All nine tournaments, from 1991 to 2023, with host country, winner and runner-up.
  • Six teams in full: Brazil, the United States, Germany, Norway, Japan and Spain, with their history at every World Cup.
  • The squad players of those six teams at the 2023 World Cup, the most recent edition, with position, date of birth, awards and previous clubs.

Other tournaments' squads and more teams will be added once the 2023 data is complete.

How it is built

Node.js and Express provide a REST API. The data lives in a MySQL database. The pages use Bootstrap 5 and load their data asynchronously from the API, with a mobile-first responsive design.
